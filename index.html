Frequently asked
My order is late — what should I do?›
If your order is more than 10 minutes past the ETA, you can chat directly with your courier from the tracking screen. If you need further help, contact our 24/7 support — we'll either rush your order or refund you in full.
Something is missing from my order›
Open the order in your history, tap "Report an issue" and select the missing items. We'll refund the missing items within 24 hours — no questions asked the first time.
How do I cancel an order?›
You can cancel for free within 60 seconds of placing the order from your Orders screen. After that, cancellation may not be possible if the restaurant has started preparing.
When will my refund arrive?›
Skyde credit appears instantly. Refunds to your card take 3–5 business days depending on your bank.
How does Skyde+ work?›
Skyde+ is our premium membership: free delivery on orders over $15, priority support, and exclusive deals. $9.99/month, cancel anytime.
Can I tip my courier after delivery?›
Yes — you have up to 24 hours after delivery to add or adjust your tip from the completed order page.
